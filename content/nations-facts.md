# Nation cards — verified data and sourcing

Every nation in the game is a real UN member state. Its **roll speed** comes from its
SDG Index rank, and its **resources** (which goals it can clear in one question) come from
real headline indicators, each listed below with its value, year and source.

## Thresholds used to award a "resource"

A nation clears that goal in **one** question if it meets the threshold; otherwise it needs **two**.

| SDG | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Poverty at $3.00/day under 5% |
| 2 | Undernourishment under 5% |
| 3 | Life expectancy 73 years or more |
| 4 | Literacy / completion 95% or more |
| 5 | Women in parliament 30% or more |
| 6 | Basic sanitation 95% or more |
| 7 | Renewable electricity 55% or more |
| 8 | Sustained growth with low unemployment (judgement) |
| 9 | Internet use 80% or more |
| 10 | Gini 0.35 or lower |
| 11 | PM2.5 at or near the WHO guideline of 5 µg/m³ (≤6) |
| 12 | High-income consumption economies fail this (SDR-N) |
| 13 | CO₂ under 4 tonnes per person |
| 14 | Marine protected area 10% or more |
| 15 | Red List Index 0.9 or higher, and no major deforestation flag |
| 16 | Corruption Perceptions Index 50 or higher |
| 17 | Meets development-finance and multilateral commitments (judgement) |

## Notes on judgement calls

- **High-income countries fail SDGs 2, 12 and 15 here**, not on the raw indicators above.
  The SDR scores wealthy countries on obesity, sustainable nitrogen use, material footprint
  and the biodiversity loss embodied in their imports — where they rate red — rather than on
  undernourishment. Applied to Finland, Germany, Poland, Japan, Chile and the United States. (SDR-N)
- **SDG 8 and SDG 17 are judgement calls.** SDG 8 is awarded where there is a documented
  strength: high-income economies with low unemployment, plus Viet Nam, Indonesia, India,
  Bangladesh, Côte d'Ivoire (sustained growth) and Kenya (mobile-money financial inclusion).
  SDG 17 goes only to established development donors — Finland, Germany, Poland, Japan.
- **No nation is awarded SDG 12.** That matches the SDR: no country on Earth is on track
  for responsible consumption and production.
- **Brazil fails SDG 15** despite a Red List Index of 0.9, because of Amazon deforestation.
- **Chad and Jordan are effectively landlocked** for SDG 14 purposes; neither is awarded it.
- **Literacy data is unusable for several countries.** The OWID series for Finland, Germany,
  Japan, the United States, Poland, Fiji and Kenya is from 1950–2000. High-income countries
  are credited with SDG 4 on well-established near-universal completion; Fiji and Kenya are
  not credited either way.
- **Marine protection in Fiji (0.9%)** counts only formally designated MPAs and misses Fiji's
  extensive community-managed *qoliqoli* areas.

## Rank and resource count measure different things

A nation's **roll speed** tracks its overall SDG Index score. Its **resource count** tracks a
narrow slice of 17 headline indicators. These can disagree: Kenya ranks 106th but scores only
3 of 17 here, because it fails on sanitation, internet access and life expectancy while the
full SDG Index rewards it across many more measures. That is a limitation of the game's proxy,
not an error in the data — and it is worth saying out loud to students who spot it.

## Personas and nations share a world

Nine of the ten persona cards are set in countries that are now playable nations: Amara in
Côte d'Ivoire, Rafael in Brazil, Priya in Bangladesh, Joseph in Poland, Nadia in Kenya,
Tomás in Peru, Mei in Indonesia, Yusuf in Jordan and Kwame in DR Congo. A team can end up
playing the nation their persona actually lives in.

## Deliberately surprising, and true

- The **United States emits 14.2 tonnes of CO₂ per person; Chad emits 0.1** — 142 times less.
- **DR Congo's electricity is 100% renewable**, and **Kenya's is 90%** — both higher than
  Germany (59.1%) or Finland (56.5%). DR Congo ranks 163rd of 169.
- **Peru's parliament is 41.5% women** — more than Germany, Japan or the United States.
  **Chad's is 33.5%**, more than Germany, Japan or Brazil. **Japan's is 15.7%.**
- **Fiji has the fewest women MPs here at 9.1%** — 5 seats of 55.
- **Bangladesh's air carries 65.0 µg/m³ of PM2.5** — thirteen times the WHO guideline.
- **Brazil's electricity is 86.6% renewable**, far ahead of any European nation here.
- The **world's #1 country still fails four of the seventeen goals.**
